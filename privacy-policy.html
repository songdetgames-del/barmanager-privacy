Bar Manager - Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 29, 2026

This policy applies to the Android game Bar Manager (the "App"), published by SongdetGames ("we", "us"). It explains what data the App handles, how it is used and with whom it may be shared.

1. Summary

The App has no account system and we do not ask for your name, email address or any other personal information. Your game progress is stored on your device only. The App may show optional rewarded video ads served by Google AdMob and may offer in-app purchases processed by Google Play. These two services handle some data as described below.

2. Data stored on your device

To let you continue your game, the App saves your progress (money, level, stock, settings, chosen language, decorations, virtual currencies and similar game data) in the local storage of the App on your device. This data is not sent to us. It is deleted when you clear the App's data or uninstall the App. You can also export or import your save file from the game settings.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App may offer optional rewarded video ads: you choose to watch an ad in exchange for an in-game reward. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may collect and process, on its own behalf, data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and operating system information, and your interactions with ads. We do not receive this data and do not use it ourselves.

Where required (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the App asks for your consent before showing personalised ads. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation at any time in your Android settings (Settings, Google, Ads). More information: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

4. In-app purchases (Google Play)

The App may offer optional in-app purchases of virtual items (such as gems or tickets). Payments are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your card or payment details. We only receive confirmation from Google Play that a purchase was completed, in order to deliver the item to you. Google's handling of your data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Data we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera data, precise location or any account information. We do not sell your data.

6. Data sharing

We do not share personal data with anyone. The only third parties involved are Google AdMob (advertising) and Google Play (payments), acting as described in sections 3 and 4.

7. Children

The App is a management game that depicts a bar and is intended for users aged 18 and over (or the legal age in their country for such content). It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Security

Your game data stays on your device, so its protection depends on the security of your device. Ad and payment data are protected by Google under its own security practices.

9. Your choices

You can decline to watch ads, decline to make purchases, reset your advertising ID, clear the App's data or uninstall the App at any time.

10. Changes to this policy

If the App changes in a way that affects data handling, we will update this page and the effective date above.

11. Contact
